DA FON at interpack 2026

Design for DaFon 大豐環保

Scope
Exhibition Graphics, Brochure Design, Illustration
Designer
Olga Yang
Date
Mar, 2026

大豐環保參加 2026 德國杜塞道夫 interpack 國際包裝展的整套參展視覺，包含展位背板、8 頁產品型錄與展場提袋。以「100% PCR 再生材料」為主軸，三件物件共用深綠主色、圓形元素與再生容器插圖，在展場上維持一致的品牌形象。

A complete exhibition kit for DA FON at interpack 2026 in Düsseldorf, Germany: booth graphics, an 8-page product brochure and a giveaway tote bag, tied together by one green palette and a set of recycled-packaging illustrations.
